Anchorline Logistics

Services

Road, sea and air freight, warehousing, cold chain and customs, run from one dispatch desk.

01 · Road freight

Line-haul and palletised distribution

Nightly line-haul between Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth, with regional runs into the Hunter, Riverina and Gold Coast. Freight is scanned at each changeover, so a delay on one leg does not become a mystery on the next.

  • Single pallets through to full trailer loads
  • Tail-lift, forklift and hand unload options
  • Dangerous goods to ADG Code, segregated on dedicated decks

02 · Warehousing

Storage, pick and pack

Racked selective storage, bulk floor space and bonded areas for goods still under customs control. Stock is counted on a rolling cycle rather than one painful stocktake a year, and your file is reconciled every Friday.

  • Inbound container unpack and put-away within 24 hours
  • Pick, pack, kitting and returns processing
  • Bonded and general storage on the same site

03 · Cold chain

Chilled and frozen freight

Two chambers at two to eight degrees and one at minus eighteen, with probes logging every fifteen minutes. The log travels with the consignment, so the temperature history is part of the delivery record rather than a separate email.

  • Continuous temperature logging on every leg
  • HACCP aligned handling procedures
  • Breach alerts to your nominated contact within ten minutes

04 · International

Sea, air and customs

Full and part container sea freight, air uplift when the deadline will not move, and licensed customs brokerage so entries are lodged before the vessel berths. We quote landed cost, not just the freight leg.

  • FCL and LCL consolidation through Port Botany
  • Licensed customs brokerage and quarantine liaison
  • Duty, GST and landed cost estimates before you commit
